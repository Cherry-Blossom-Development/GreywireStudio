Reading the diagram

Hole names: the letter is the row (a–e top half, f–j bottom half), the number is the column. Any hole in the same column and half works the same, so the exact letter only matters for keeping parts from crowding each other.

Some boards put the red and blue rails in the opposite order. Follow the colors, not the positions. Unplug the power before changing anything.
